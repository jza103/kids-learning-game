# Kids Learning Game (working title)

**Status:** spelling game and times tables game live on GitHub Pages
**Created:** 2026-11-17  **Last updated:** 2026-11-21 (mining theme 2026-11-20)

## Summary
An iPad-friendly learning game for my kids. Parent loads weekly spelling words; kids master them through adaptive spaced repetition wrapped in a game. Also drills math facts (multiplication/division tables). Long-term: tie into the published curriculum and generate lessons/games dynamically.

## Running it
- No build step and no dependencies: plain HTML/CSS/ES modules.
- Tests (spaced repetition, coins, packs, battle): `npm test` (Node 18+).
- Try locally: `npm start`, then open http://localhost:8080.
- Hosting: `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes to GitHub Pages on every push to `main` (needs Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions; a private repo needs GitHub Pro for Pages). Site: https://jza103.github.io/kids-learning-game/
- On the iPad: open the site in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. After the first load it works offline.
- Grown-ups screen: press and hold the 👪 button on the home or hub screen.

## How the spelling game works (MVP)
- **Players:** one profile per kid on the shared iPad, each with their own word list and progress.
- **Word bank:** each week the parent adds about 10 words (`word | example sentence`). They join a growing bank and become "this week's" words. Older words keep their 0–5 star mastery rating, and due ones come back for review: about 20% of picks, at most 3 per round, more when this week's words are all caught up. Adding an old word again puts it back in this week's set.
- **Practice:** Leitner boxes 0–5 (`src/srs.js`). Words are read aloud; missed and low-box words are picked more often. A miss costs 2 stars and the kid copies the word once to "lock it in". Mastered = box 5.
- **Practice over time (2026-11-20, Jason):** the first 2 stars can come in one round; from then on a spelling or Word Match word earns at most one new star a day (the round says "Next ⭐ for this word tomorrow" and the word waits until tomorrow), so a list takes at least 4 days to master and a one-day cram doesn't pay. Times tables are exempt (no cap on math, Jason). `STAR_RULES` in `src/srs.js`. In the year sim, a Saturday-only crammer went from 24 Week Stars to 4 and from really knowing 60% of the words to 87%; a daily player went from about 11–15 Week Stars to 8, with the same Legendaries.
- **Game: ⛏️ Gem Dig.** 10 words; each right word is a pickaxe swing that cracks a rock (3 in a row swings harder), and a cracked rock gives a gem (Rose Quartz to Diamond). A miss lets 1 rubble slide back (a shield friend's support beam stops it). No timers, no losing.
- **Mining theme (2026-11-20, Jason):** the three learning games are different mining activities instead of one monster battle: ⛏️ Gem Dig (spelling), 🪣 Ore Sorting (Word Match: word chunks into meaning crates, filling ore buckets) and 🛒 Mine Cart Run (times tables: the cart races to the next station; time trials are Cart races and the Final mission is the 🚂 Mother Lode Express). Presentation only: `src/battle.js` still runs the rounds with the old numbers (targets of size 4–7, `MINE_TARGETS` in `src/content.js`), so XP, coins and friend powers are unchanged. Look and wording live in `src/mine.js`.
- **Coins** (`src/economy.js`): only from correct answers, by box before answering (15/12/10/8/5/2), +5 for getting a recently missed word right, half for hinted answers.
- **Mastery = big rewards:** the first time a word reaches 5 stars, it pays 30 coins and 50 XP, and every 5 mastered words earn a **Super Pack** with a guaranteed **ultra** friend (2 per theme, 10 in all; not craftable, not in normal packs, not limited by the daily cap).
- **School-year rewards (2026-11-20, Jason picked plan A):** a new 12-friend theme each month through June (`from` dates in `src/content.js`; 12 themes, 144 friends by June). Super Packs give the chosen theme's ultras, then ✨ gold versions (+1 power) of owned rares/epics/ultras, then other ultras, then gold anywhere. 🏆 Legendary friends (34, `LEGENDS`) only come from finished sets: a Table Titan per table 3–12 (all 20 × and ÷ facts mastered), one per 4 Week Stars (a whole week's list of 5+ words mastered; since 2026-11-20 any week's list counts, even when it's finished after the next list arrives), one per 25 mastered Word Match words (`src/legend.js`). Shards never buy ultras, gold or Legendaries. Proposal and pacing math: [Friend rewards for the school year](https://claude.ai/artifact/1YJkrcn1zCmz8qivuDWEmo).
- **Mastery refresh (2026-11-20):** mastered words and facts come back after 7, 14, 30, then 60 days (half that for items missed 2+ times) and show 4 stars + 🧽 ("needs a polish": the gem lost its shine) until answered right; each polish gives gem dust (`REFRESH_DAYS` in `src/srs.js`). Rewards are paid once.
- **Math mastery has no waiting (2026-11-20, Jason):** every in-time Speed answer adds a star, so a kid can master facts as fast as they can; early repeats still pay only 2 coins.
- **Coin pace (2026-11-20):** a tester earned 1,000+ coins in 30 minutes, mostly from times tables (hundreds of new facts, each paying 15, then 12 and 10 as it comes back the same session). Now every game shares a daily pace: the first 300 coins from answers pay in full, then half up to 500, then 1 coin per answer up to 600, then the coin bag is full until tomorrow (XP and friend XP still count). Mastery rewards always pay in full. Numbers in `COIN_PACE`/`DAILY_COIN_MAX` in `src/economy.js`.
- **Packs:** cost rises through the day: 60, 60, 80, 100, 120 (420 for all 5), back to 60 the next day (`PACK_COSTS`). At most 5 per day (was 3 until 2026-11-20, kids found it too few), kid picks a theme (Ocean, Space, Forest, Farm, Sweets; 12 friends each: 6 common, 3 rare, 1 epic, 2 ultra; 60 in all). Odds shown (65/28/7%). Rare+ guaranteed every 4 packs, epic every 12. Missing friends come before duplicates; duplicates give shards; shards craft any missing friend, so every set can be finished.
- **Voice:** optional natural AI voice from ElevenLabs (Jason's choice, 2026-11-19). The parent pastes an API key on the grown-ups screen; the key stays on the device and is left out of backups. Audio for each word and sentence is fetched once, saved in Cache Storage (`spelling-audio`, kept across app updates), and played offline. Each request carries spelling-test context (`previous_text`), the word-sentence-word playback is one clip so the word sounds the same each time, and the voice is tuned steady and a little slow (`CLIP_STYLE` in `src/aivoice.js`; bump it to re-make clips). Audio no word needs any more is cleaned up. Anything without saved audio falls back to the best built-in voice. Note: iPad Safari doesn't expose downloaded Premium voices to web pages (WebKit bug 237584). Background: `docs/voice-options.md`.
- **Collectibles have a purpose:** up to 3 on your team give powers (hints, damage, shields, XP boost, friend boost). Finishing a theme gives a title and XP.
- **Friends grow** (`src/friends.js`, 2026-11-20): each friend specializes in spelling, math or vocab, and its power only works in games it knows. Team friends get XP from answers (capped daily), level up, and evolve into ✨ Super Friends (level 5) and 💖 Best Friends (level 10), getting stronger. A friend brought to a game it doesn't know learns it over about a week of play. Other games plug in with `teamPowersFor` and `friendsAfterAnswer` (see `src/games.js`). Pacing math: `docs/friends-pacing.md`.
- **XP / Locker:** cosmetic only (hero, hat, color, title).

## Tricky words list (grades 5–6)
- For a kid who no longer gets school spelling words (Jason's 5th grader, 2026-11-20). 200 commonly misspelled and tricky words in 20 sets of 10, each set sharing a pattern (ough words, i before e, sound-alikes like their/there/they're, -able/-ible, silent letters...) with a sentence per word and a spelling tip per set. Later sets are 6th-grade and stretch words. List in `src/tricky-words.js`, logic in `src/tricky.js` (tested in `test/tricky.test.js`).
- Grown-ups screen: a checkbox per kid. Turning it on adds set 1 as this week's words; after that the next set is added the first time the kid opens the game a week or more later (never more than one at a time, so a break doesn't pile up words). "Add the next set now" skips ahead.
- The words are normal word-bank words: same stars, coins, mastery rewards and Super Packs, and older sets come back for review. No slowdown for fast learners: once every word of this week's set has 3+ stars, the kid sees a "Ready for 10 more tricky words" button on the home screen.

## How the times tables game works
- **Facts:** × and ÷ tables 0–12 (169 × facts, 156 ÷ facts, no ÷0). Each fact has its own 0–5 star rating, using the same scheduler as spelling words (`src/math.js`, stored in `profile.math`).
- **Modes:** Multiply, Divide, and Mixed (advanced). Kids pick "Smart mix" or any single table. Smart mix starts with 0, 1, 2, 10, 5 and opens the next table when 80% of open facts have 2+ stars. At most 5 new facts per round.
- **Practice vs Speed:** Practice has no timer, pays half coins and XP, and can take a fact only up to 3 stars. Speed gives a time per question (6 s default; the grown-ups screen offers 4–15 s). A right answer in time adds a star; a right answer after the timer still moves the cart but counts like a hinted answer (no star, half coins). So 5 stars means fast recall.
- **Warm-ups (2026-11-20):** any fact with a 0, 1 or 2 (5 × 0, 7 × 1, 14 ÷ 2, 12 ÷ 6) is a warm-up: no coins, at most 2 stars (so Smart mix still opens tables), never mastered, half friend XP. A tester ground the 0s for coins. A warm-up at its 2 stars rests for 30 days and Smart mix skips resting warm-ups while there are other facts (`WARMUP_REST_DAYS`); before that, the year sim found 45% of a daily player's math answers went to warm-ups, now 24%, and steady players master all 200 facts instead of about 60%. The facts that count are the 100 × and 100 ÷ facts with both numbers 3–12 (`isWarmup`, `trackedFacts` in `src/math.js`).
- **Anti-grind:** Repeating a fact before it's due still adds a star (no waiting) but pays only 2 coins. Mastering a fact pays 15 coins once, and every 2 mastered facts fill one Super Pack dot (shared with spelling).
- **Play:** 12-question Mine Cart Run with an on-screen number pad (keyboard digits and Enter work on a computer). A miss shows the right fact big and what the kid typed small and crossed out, then they type the answer once to lock it in. Hint = a skip-counting clue.
- **Friend hooks:** `mathPowers()` and `afterMathAnswer()` at the top of `src/mathgame.js` are where game-type-aware friend powers plug in.
- **Time trials:** race every fact of one table (× , ÷ or mixed) against a stopwatch; a miss adds 3 s and must be fixed. Each table keeps a personal best (`profile.math.records`, key like `mul:7`). The 🏁 Final mission takes one random fact from every × table (0–12) and ÷ table (1–12), 25 facts, with its own record. A new best gives 30 XP (cosmetic). Personal bests only, never compared between kids. First tries in a trial count for stars and coins like Speed mode.
- **Solve times:** every right answer records how long it took; each fact keeps a running average (newest answer weighs 30%, pauses capped at 20 s). "My facts" and the grown-ups screen list the slowest facts.
- **Kid view:** "My facts" shows color-coded × and ÷ charts. Grown-ups screen shows mastered facts per table, tricky facts and the timer setting.

## How Word Match (vocabulary) works
- **Game:** tap a word, then tap what it means. No typing. A round is 3 boards of 4 words, each with one extra meaning so the last match isn't a free guess. Each match drops a nugget into the ore bucket (same round rules as spelling). New words get a quick "New words!" card first and come in at most 6 per round. Works on iPhone (words in a 2×2 grid above the meanings), iPad and desktop (side by side).
- **Words:** each kid starts with 44 third-grade starter words (`src/vocab-words.js`, no synonym pairs so boards stay fair). Grown-ups add words with separate Word and Meaning boxes, ✎ edits, ✕ deletes, and "Put back deleted starter words" restores them. Words the parent adds come in before the remaining starter words.
- **Rewards:** only the first try at each word counts. It gets the same 0–5 stars as spelling (its own list, `profile.vocab`), pays 70% of the spelling coin table (`VOCAB_COIN_SCALE` in `src/vocab.js`), and mastering a word pays the usual mastery coins/XP and counts toward Super Packs. Team powers: 💡 hint takes away a wrong meaning; shield and power work as in spelling.
- Code: `src/vocab.js` (logic, tested in `test/vocab.test.js`), `src/vocab-ui.js` (screens and grown-ups editor, plugged into `app.js`).

## Friend Grove (farm side game)
- **What:** a farm where every friend has a job. Open it from the hub (🌻). Friends work on 5 crews (Fields, Pens, Forage, Workshop, Build). Crops grow, wild critters visit and move in for their favorite crop, critters make goodies, and the workshop crafts. Friends post requests that pay **Grove Bells** (the farm's own money), which buy plots, pens, new lands and decorations.
- **Learning powers it (round 2, 2026-11-21):** every correct answer in any game puts ☀️ **Sunshine** in the kid's Sun Jar (`src/sunshine.js`): 1, or 2 for a word or fact missed last time, plus 10 for mastering one. Warm-up facts give none. Pouring the jar fills every crop, craft and expedition at the crew's speed at pour time (so adding a Grower later still helps) and moves `farm.clock` for goodies and forage, so everything grows from learning, not wall-clock time. Nothing depends on logging in, and pouring stops at what's useful. Before pouring, the farm warns about empty plots, an empty Fields crew, an idle workshop or a free expedition team ("Pour anyway" / "Not yet"). There's 8 free Sunshine (Morning Rain) on the first visit each day. Round summaries show ☀️ earned, and the hub button shows what's waiting.
- **Golden Season:** when every spelling word, counted math fact and Word Match word is mastered and none needs a refresh, the farm grows on its own (1 Sunshine per 10 minutes, up to 48 per visit). Jason: no gate for a kid who has mastered everything.
- **Every friend matters:** each friend has a job (Grower, Tender, Gatherer, Crafter, Builder) and an element (Sun, Water, Earth, Frost, Glow, Wind), worked out in `src/farm-content.js`, so new monthly themes get them automatically. Traits show on collection cards. Working its own job counts double. Super/Best Friend, gold, ultra and legendary friends work harder, and rare-and-up friends have a once-a-day perk. Crews have 6 spots to start. Each area is expanded with Grove Bells (50/110/190/300, Builder discount applies), gated by farm level 2/3/5/8, up to 5 per area (25 total). **Paced over the school year (2026-11-20, Jason):** each land arrives with a monthly theme (Frost Ridge 4 Jan, Tide Pools 1 Mar, Glow Caves 5 Apr, Cloud Garden 3 May; `from` in `BIOMES`) and still needs its farm level; each farm level costs 25 XP more than the last (level 15 = 4,375 XP, `farmXpForLevel`); the market buys up to 200 Bells a day (`MARKET_BELLS`; requests always pay); expeditions pay 1/3 shards (was 3/8). In the year sim a daily player used to have everything built by week 8 with about 2 million Bells earned; now the last land opens in May (week 23) with about 42,000 Bells earned for about 6,500 to spend. Farm level no longer adds spots by itself; farms from before round 3 keep the spots they had.
- **Friends tab, energy and know-how (round 4):** the 👥 Friends tab lists every friend with job, element, where it is, rank and ⚡ energy, with one-tap buttons to move it to any area or rest it, and filters (Working / Tired / Resting / Exploring). Working uses 1 energy per Sunshine poured and earns 1 farm XP for that friend; resting friends recover 3 per Sunshine, everyone gets +30 each morning, and trips cost 20/40 energy and give the team 15/40 XP. Ranks: New Hand, Helper (30), Skilled (90), Expert (200), Master (400), +1 power at Skilled and +2 at Master. 20 Sunshine worked in every area gives the All-Rounder medal (+1 power). A tired friend still works at half power and learns nothing, so there's a reason to rotate but nothing is lost. This farm XP is separate from learning friend XP (evolutions).
- **Expeditions (🗺️ tab):** friends not on a crew go exploring in teams of 3 (short trip, 15 ☀️) or 6 (big trip, 40 ☀️). There are 2 teams at once, 3 at farm level 5 and 4 at level 10. Each of the 5 lands has 3 routes (2 short, 1 big), all available every day with new needs (elements, jobs or themes; each need met is +25% speed and more treasure). Every route has its own treasure and souvenir, and each big route has its own explorer critter (guaranteed after 3 big trips there), so finishing the Explore journal page means opening every land. Rewards also include Grove Bells, 💎 3 or 8 shards and a story card naming the team. Map pieces were removed (round 3).
- **Wishing Well (Packs screen):** pick an element or job and get a friend you don't have yet with it, never an ultra or Legendary. Pay with coins (pack price + 20, uses one of the day's packs, rarity rolled like a pack) or with shards (pick the rarity: 15 / 45 / 110 💎). This replaced Jason's 2-for-1 idea because duplicates come too rarely.
- **Shards come from:** duplicate pack pulls (5 / 15 / 40), a Super Pack when everything is owned (150), refreshing a mastered word or fact (2), and expeditions (3 / 8). They're spent on the Wishing Well, crafting a specific friend, crystal farm decorations and fertilizer.
- **Learning still drives friends:** the farm never pays coins, packs or friends. Each mastery also gives a 🌟 Wonder Seed, and each Legendary or finished theme gives a 🥚 Mystery Egg. Farm XP comes from Sunshine poured (1 XP each) plus a few one-off firsts (a new critter, a Wonder crop), so farm progress follows learning, capped at 100 a day (harvest, goody, craft and request XP were dropped 2026-11-20: one Sunshine grows every plot, so a kid with no learning reached level 62 on Morning Rain alone); farm level gates expansions, lands and expedition teams, so collecting everything takes weeks. Learning itself is never capped.
- **Siblings:** send up to 5 of one item to a brother or sister on the same device, once a day each. **Parent setting:** "Only open Friend Grove after a learning game that day", per kid, off by default.
- Code: `src/farm-content.js`, `src/farm.js` (rules, tested in `test/farm.test.js`), `src/farmui.js` (screen), `src/sunshine.js`; the Wishing Well is in `src/economy.js`.

## School-year simulator (`sim/`)
- Plays whole school years with randomized kids of 8 play styles (steady, weekend crammer, grinder, Grove only, Grove fan, early master, never-Grove, on-and-off) through the game's real code, with a forgetting model for what each kid really knows. `node sim/run.js` prints a table per style; `node sim/exploits.js` checks known exploits. How to run and change it: `sim/README.md`.
- Mastery deleted and re-added on the grown-ups screen (spelling words, Word Match words) no longer pays its rewards twice (`masteryPaid`).
- Star rules for spelling and Word Match: `STAR_RULES` in `src/srs.js` (on since 2026-11-20); `node sim/run.js --rules dailyFrom=null,missDrop=5` replays the old rules.

## Hypothesis / what we're trying to prove
- Kids will happily practice if the *game* is fun, without needing manipulative monetization.
- Spaced repetition (adaptive scheduling of missed items) beats flat quizzes for spelling + math facts.
- Weekly word-list loading is low-friction enough for a parent to sustain.

## Inspiration & recon (light, not vetted first-hand)
See `recon.md`. Short version: kids like Blooket (collection/reveal rewards, game variety) and Prodigy (RPG progression). Prodigy was the target of a 2021 FTC complaint over membership pressure on kids. We want the fun, not the dark patterns.

## Decisions
- **Platform: PWA** (installable on iPad via Safari, no App Store, offline via service worker, TTS via Web Speech API). (2026-11-17)
- **Progress storage: on-device only** for now (IndexedDB/localStorage; no accounts or sync). Consider export/import backup later since iOS can evict PWA storage. (2026-11-17)
- **Curriculum:** believed to be district-wide; user expects to download it and hand the files to Claude. Defer until MVP works.

- **Balance:** game-heavy over learning is intentional; it must compete with Blooket/Prodigy. (2026-11-17)
- **Hard no:** purchases/ads/upsell, streak-loss guilt, leaderboards between kids.
- **Randomized rewards: decided (2026-11-19)** after Jason tested Blooket with the kids: collectibles with rarity via packs, kept mild. Odds shown, pity guarantees, duplicates → shards, daily cap, kid picks a theme, collectibles give in-game powers and fill a collection book, coins only from correct answers (missed words pay more, mastered less), parent writes the content. Background in `appeal-analysis.md`.
